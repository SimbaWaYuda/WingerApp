# Winger user guide

Open the shop at [https://wingermarket.com](https://wingermarket.com) or in the Winger Android app. On the welcome screen you can switch the language to English, Español, or Kiswahili.

Use **Shop as a customer** to buy. Use **Become a Supplier** to sell.

## Customers: from login to checkout

### 1. Create an account

1. On the welcome screen, tap **New here? Create an account**.
2. Leave **Shop as a customer** selected.
3. Enter your full name, email address, and a password.
4. Tap **Create account**.

To return later, choose **Shop as a customer**, enter the same email and password, and tap **Continue**.

If the screen says the API is offline, wait a moment and tap **Continue** again.

### 2. Find a product

1. **Home** shows the catalogue. The number of products across the screen follows the size of your phone or window.
2. Use **Search** to look up a name, such as shoes or a brand.
3. Tap a product to open it.

The heart saves it to your wishlist. The cart icon adds it with the colour and size currently selected.

### 3. Choose colour and size

On the product page:

1. Under **Select colour**, tap a colour. The large photo changes to the picture saved for that colour.
2. Under **Select size**, tap a size. Sizes that are out of stock cannot be added.
3. Set the quantity.
4. Choose **Express 1–2 days**, **Standard 3–5 days**, or **Pickup**.
5. Tap **Add to cart**.

The cart line shows the product with the colour and size you picked, for example `black · 39`.

### 4. Check out

1. Open **Cart**.
2. Check the supplier, the colour and size, and the total.
3. Tap **Checkout all products**.

Checkout has five steps:

1. **Review.** Confirm the items, then tap **Continue**.
2. **Address.** Enter the street or area, the city, and a phone number the supplier can call. The phone needs at least 7 digits. Tap **Continue**.
3. **Delivery.** Choose express, standard, or pickup. The delivery fee and tax are shown before you continue.
4. **Payment.** Choose **Card** or **Pay on delivery**, then tap **Place order**.
5. **Confirmation.** You receive an order number, such as `WG-10118`.

Pay on delivery means you pay the supplier when the order arrives.

### 5. Follow the order

Open **Orders** and tap the order. You will see the product, the colour and size, the delivery address, and the shipment status. Status moves from processing, to shipped or ready for pickup, to delivered.

## Suppliers: from a new account to shipment

### 1. Create a supplier account

1. On the welcome screen, tap **New here? Create an account**.
2. Select **Become a Supplier**.
3. Enter your full name, email address, and password.
4. Enter the **Business name**. This is the name shoppers see on your products.
5. The preferred supplier id is optional. Leave it blank to let Winger assign one.
6. Tap **Create account**.

Next time, choose **Become a Supplier** on the welcome screen, sign in, and tap **Continue**.

### 2. Add a product

1. Open **Products**.
2. Tap **Add product**.
3. Add photos. You can add up to 8 images: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF, at least 600×600 pixels, and no larger than 5 MB. Use your own photos.
4. Enter the name, brand, price, and stock.
5. Save the product.

### 3. Add colours and sizes

Keep one product. Do not create a separate product for every colour.

1. On the product, tap **Product options**.
2. Add one row for each combination. Example: `Black` and `39`, then `Black` and `40`, then `White` and `39`.
3. Enter the stock for that exact colour and size.
4. A colour hex such as `#1A1A1A` sets the colour of the dot. It is optional when the colour name is a common one such as black, white, or brown.
5. Save.

Use the same colour name every time. `Black` and `black and brown` are different colours to the shop. The photo shown to the shopper is the one saved for that exact colour name.

**Receive +20** adds twenty units to the product when you are not tracking each colour and size separately.

### 4. Set delivery

Open **Delivery fees** and set how you deliver:

- You deliver the order yourself, or
- A courier delivers it. Enter the courier name when you choose a courier.

Shoppers then see express, standard, or pickup, and the fee for your products.

### 5. Read a new order

Open **Orders** when a shopper checks out. Each line shows:

- The product name
- The colour and size the shopper chose
- The customer’s phone number, which you can tap to call
- Whether they will pay by card or on delivery

### 6. Ship the order

1. Tap **Fulfillment**.
2. Choose **Ship** or **Pickup**.
3. For a shipment you handle yourself, tap **Mark shipped**. For a courier order, tap **Book courier**, then add the courier fee and tracking link under **Courier details**.
4. When the customer has the item, tap **Mark delivered**.
5. If the order is pay on delivery, tap **Collect payment** after you receive the money.

Pickup orders are marked ready for the customer to collect, then **Mark delivered** when they take them.

If you cannot send an item, tap **Cannot fulfil** while it is still processing. After a return, use **Returns** or **Create replacement**.
